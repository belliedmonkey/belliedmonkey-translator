What a long walk teaches about pace

The first hour always feels too slow, and that is exactly the point: a pace you can hold for six hours is never the pace that feels fast at the start.

Walkers who begin at a comfortable speed tend to arrive earlier than those who sprint the first mile, because the cost of recovering from an early mistake is paid over the whole afternoon.

长途步行教给人的节奏

以舒服的速度出发的人，往往比头一英里就冲刺的人更早到达，因为一开始犯的错，代价要用整个下午来还。
