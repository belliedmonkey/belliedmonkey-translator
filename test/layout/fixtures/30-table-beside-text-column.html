The giant panda is a bear species endemic to China, characterised by its white coat with black patches around the eyes, ears, legs and shoulders, and it feeds almost entirely on bamboo and bamboo shoots throughout the year.

Females rear cubs for an average of eighteen to twenty four months, and the oldest known individual of the species lived to thirty eight years of age while in captivity.
