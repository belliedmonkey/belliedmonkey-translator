Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free and open source. Google translation works out of the box with no key.

Which translation engines are supported?

Google (free, no key), plus OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), DeepSeek, and Zhipu (GLM) using your own API key. You choose in Settings.

Where do I put my API key?

Open the app's Settings, pick your provider, and paste the key. It is stored only on your device and sent only to that provider when translating.

What can it translate?

  • Web pages — bilingual view: the original paragraph with the translation shown right below it.
  • YouTube — dual subtitles: original on top, translation underneath.

How do I turn it on?

Enable the extension in Safari settings, then use the floating button on the page (or the in-player button on YouTube) to start translating.

Do you collect my data?

No. See the Privacy Policy. There is no server operated by us; nothing is collected.
