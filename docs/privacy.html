Privacy Policy

BelliedMonkey Translator · Last updated 5 July 2026

Short version: We do not collect, store, sell, or share any of your data. The app has no server operated by us — there is nowhere for your data to go. It is fully open source.

1. Information we collect

None. BelliedMonkey Translator has no backend server, no account system, no analytics, and no advertising or tracking SDKs. We, the developer, never receive any information about you or your usage.

2. Data stored on your device

The following are stored only locally on your device, in your browser's extension storage, and never transmitted to us:

  • Your settings (chosen translation provider, target language, display options).
  • The API key you enter, if you use a provider that requires one. It is kept on your device and sent only to that provider when you translate.
  • A temporary translation cache (to avoid re-translating the same text), which expires automatically.

Uninstalling the app or clearing the extension's storage removes all of this.

3. Third-party translation services

To translate, the text you choose is sent directly from your device, over HTTPS, to the translation provider you select — using your own API key where applicable. We are not part of that connection and never see the text.

That text is processed under the privacy policy of the provider you pick:

  • Google (default free translation)
  • OpenAI
  • Anthropic (Claude)
  • DeepSeek
  • Zhipu (GLM)

Please review the policy of whichever provider you use. You can change or remove your provider at any time in the app's settings.

4. No tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites, do not build advertising profiles, and do not use third-party analytics.

5. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Because the app is open source, its full history is public.

7. Contact

Questions? Email belliedmonkey@gmail.com or open an issue on GitHub.

隐私政策（简体中文）

大肚猴翻译 · 最后更新 2026 年 7 月 5 日

一句话：我们不收集、存储、出售或分享你的任何数据。本 App 没有我们运营的服务器——你的数据无处可去。项目完全开源。

我们收集的信息：无。没有后端服务器、没有账号系统、没有统计分析、没有广告或追踪。开发者不会收到任何关于你的信息。

本机存储：你的设置、（如需要的）API 密钥、以及会自动过期的翻译缓存，仅保存在你设备的浏览器扩展存储中，不会上传给我们。卸载或清除扩展存储即全部删除。

第三方翻译服务：翻译时，你选中的文本会经 HTTPS 从你的设备直接发送给你所选择的翻译服务商（Google／OpenAI／Anthropic／DeepSeek／智谱 GLM），并按其隐私政策处理。我们不参与该连接，也看不到这些文本。你可随时在设置中更换或移除服务商。

无追踪：不做跨 App／网站追踪，不建广告画像，不用第三方统计。
