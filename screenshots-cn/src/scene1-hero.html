The Quiet Rise of On-Device AI

Modern phones now run capable language models entirely on-device, without sending data to the cloud. 如今的手机已能完全在本地运行强大的语言模型，无需将数据发送到云端。
This shift means faster responses, better privacy, and translation that keeps working even when you are offline. 这一转变带来了更快的响应、更好的隐私保护，以及即使离线也能持续工作的翻译能力。
For language learners, reading foreign articles side by side with a translation is a powerful way to build vocabulary. 对语言学习者而言，将外文文章与译文并排阅读，是积累词汇的有力方式。
